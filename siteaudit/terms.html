By paying a deposit, an invoice, or a subscription, you agree to them. If something in your written quote or invoice is different from these terms, the quote or invoice wins.

2. Payment

  • One-time fixes and rebuilds: 50% deposit to start, 50% when the work is done and you've had a chance to review it. Final files and access are handed over once the balance is paid.
  • Care plan: billed monthly in advance through Stripe, starting on the day you sign up.
  • Invoices are due within 14 days. If an invoice is more than 30 days late, we may pause work or care-plan services until it's paid.
  • Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.

3. Refunds and cancellation

  • Before work starts: cancel and we refund your deposit in full.
  • After work starts: the deposit covers work already done and isn't refundable. If you cancel midway, you won't owe the balance, and you keep whatever is finished.
  • Care plan: cancel any time by email, phone or text (see customer support). Cancelling stops the next charge; service continues until the end of the month you've paid for. We don't refund partial months.

4. What the care plan includes

  • Software, theme, and plugin updates (where your platform allows it).
  • Security monitoring and fixing problems caused by updates.
  • Regular backups of the site where your host or platform supports them.
  • A monthly speed check, and fixes for anything that has slowed the site down.
  • Small edits — text, photo, hours, price, and menu changes — up to about 1 hour of work per month. Unused time doesn't roll over. Bigger requests get a quote first.
  • We'll reply to care-plan requests within 1 business day and complete small edits within 3 business days.

Hosting, domain names, premium plugins, and site-builder subscriptions (Wix, Squarespace, etc.) are not included. You pay those providers directly.

5. Timelines

Your quote gives an estimated number of business days. The clock starts once we have your deposit and the access we need (website login, hosting, domain). Delays in getting access, content, or feedback move the timeline by the same amount.

6. Your part

  • Give us access to your website, hosting, and domain accounts as needed. We'll only use it for the work you've hired us for. You can change passwords when we're done.
  • Make sure you have the rights to the text, photos, and logos you give us.
  • Keep your hosting, domain, and site-builder accounts paid and in your name.

7. Ownership

Once a job is paid in full, you own the website content and the custom work we did for you. Themes, plugins, fonts, and stock photos stay under their own licenses. We may show a screenshot of finished work in our portfolio unless you ask us not to.

8. Our guarantee

If something we fixed or built breaks within 30 days because of our work, we'll fix it free. This doesn't cover problems caused by later changes from you or another developer, your host or site-builder, or third-party plugins and services.

9. What we can't promise

  • Search rankings and sales. We can make your site faster and easier for Google to read, but no one can guarantee rankings, traffic, calls, or revenue.
  • Speed scores. Results depend on your host, platform, and third-party tools. Sites on builders like Wix or Squarespace have limits we can't get around; we'll tell you up front.
  • Legal compliance. Accessibility fixes address the issues our audit finds. They are not a legal certification of full ADA or WCAG compliance.

10. Limitation of liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, our total liability for any claim related to our services is limited to what you paid us for that job, or for the care plan, the last 3 months of fees. We are not liable for lost profits, lost data, or other indirect or consequential damages. We keep backups as described above, but you're responsible for keeping your own copy of anything you can't afford to lose.

11. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Colorado. Any dispute will be handled in the courts of Larimer County, Colorado.

12. Changes

We may update these terms from time to time. Changes don't apply to a job already quoted. Care-plan customers will be emailed at least 30 days before a change that affects them.
