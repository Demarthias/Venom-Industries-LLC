Customer Support

Need a change made, have a question about an invoice, or think something on your site is broken? You'll reach me, Grayson, directly. There's no ticket system or call center.

When I reply: messages are answered Monday to Friday. Anything sent on a weekend or holiday is answered the next business day. Call or text for anything urgent.

Response times

  • Site down or broken: call or text. I'll get back to you as soon as I can, and look at it the same business day whenever possible.
  • Care plan members: a reply within 1 business day, and small edits done within 3 business days.
  • Everyone else: a reply within 1–2 business days.

Asking for a change

To get your edit done in one go, include:

  • The page it's on (a link is perfect).
  • What it says now, and what it should say. Copy and paste the new text if you can.
  • Any new photos as attachments, as large as you have them. I'll resize them.
  • A screenshot if something looks wrong, and what phone or computer you saw it on.

Care plan members get up to about 1 hour of small edits a month. If a request is bigger than that, I'll send a fixed quote before doing anything, so you're never billed by surprise.

If something I fixed breaks

If something I fixed or built stops working within 30 days because of my work, I'll fix it free. Email or text me with what you're seeing. If it turns out to be caused by something else, like a host outage, a plugin update or another developer's change, I'll tell you what happened and quote the fix if you want me to handle it.

Billing and payments

  • Invoices are sent by email from Stripe and can be paid by card or bank transfer. Lost one? Email me and I'll resend it.
  • Fix jobs and new websites are half up front and half when the work is done and you've reviewed it.
  • The care plan is billed monthly on the day you signed up. Receipts come by email from Stripe.
  • Update your card by emailing me, and I'll send you a secure Stripe link. I never take card numbers by phone or email.

Cancelling the care plan

Email or text me that you'd like to cancel. You won't be charged again, and the plan stays active until the end of the month you've already paid for. There's no cancellation fee and no contract. Before you go, I'll send you every login and make sure nothing on your site depends on me.

Refunds

If you cancel a fix job or new website before work starts, your deposit is refunded in full. After work has started, the deposit covers the work already done, you won't owe the balance, and you keep whatever is finished. Care plan months aren't refunded part-way through. Full details are in the service terms.

Access and passwords

  • When possible, create a separate login for me rather than sharing your own. I can walk you through it on your platform.
  • Never email a password in the same message as the username. Text one and email the other, or use a password-sharing link.
  • When a job or care plan ends, I delete the logins I have, and you should remove my access or change the password.

Stop outreach emails

If you got an email about your website and don't want any more, reply "stop" to it, or email venomindys@gmail.com with the subject "Stop". Your address goes on a permanent do-not-contact list. See the privacy policy for what's collected and how to have it deleted.
