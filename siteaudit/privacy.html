Website Services Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026  ·  Venom-Industries LLC

These cover SiteAudit website services. Looking for SaaS Detective, the browser extension? See the SaaS Detective privacy policy.

This policy covers SiteAudit: the website audit, fix, rebuild, and care-plan services of Venom-Industries LLC ("we", "us"). It explains what we collect, why, and how to have it removed. For the SaaS Detective browser extension, see the SaaS Detective privacy policy.

1. Businesses we contact

We find local businesses through public sources like Google Maps and the businesses' own websites. From those we collect:

  • Business name, address, phone number, rating, and review count.
  • The business website, and a business email address published on it.
  • Results of an automated check of the public website (speed, mobile layout, broken links, and similar) and a screenshot of how it looks on a phone.

We use this only to tell you what we found on your website and offer to fix it. Every email includes our mailing address and a way to opt out. Reply "stop" to any email and we will never email that address again. We keep opted-out addresses on a do-not-contact list so they stay blocked.

2. Customers

When you hire us or subscribe to the care plan, we collect:

  • Your name, business name, email, phone number, and website address.
  • Billing details, which are collected and stored by Stripe. We see the last four digits of your card and your payment history, never the full card number.
  • Logins you share with us for your website, hosting, or domain. We use these only to do the work you've hired us for, store them securely, and delete them when the job or care plan ends. We recommend changing those passwords afterward.
  • Emails and messages you send us.

3. Visitors to your website

When we work on your website, we may be able to see data it already collects, such as form submissions or analytics. We only access it as needed to do the work, and we don't copy, use, or share it for any other purpose. If we install analytics or call tracking for you, the data belongs to you and lives in your account.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with services we use to run the business:

  • Stripe: payments and subscriptions.
  • Google (Gmail): sending and receiving email.
  • Anthropic: we may use AI to help draft outreach emails, using the public business information and audit results described in section 1.
  • Analytics on venom-industries.com: our own site uses PostHog and Google Tag Manager to measure visits.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

5. How long we keep it

  • Prospect data and audit results: up to 24 months, or until you ask us to delete it.
  • Do-not-contact list: permanently, so we can keep honoring your opt-out.
  • Customer records and invoices: as long as you're a customer, plus 7 years for tax and accounting records.
  • Website logins: deleted when the job or care plan ends.

6. Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete the information we have about you or your business. Email venomindys@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days. If you ask us to delete your data, we'll keep only your email on the do-not-contact list so we don't contact you again.

7. Security

We keep data on secured, password-protected systems, email is sent over encrypted connections, and access is limited to us. No system is perfectly secure, but we'll tell you promptly if a problem affects your information.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
