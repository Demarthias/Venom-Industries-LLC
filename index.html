Why I built this

I kept wanting to know what a site was running.

What software is this company actually using to manage their customers? Does this online store look legit enough to buy from, or is it going to eat my card number and vanish into thin air? I got tired of digging through page source code and squinting at little padlock icons trying to guess whether a site's security was real or just for show, so I built something that just tells me.

SaaS Detective turned out to be useful to more people than just me, which is the whole reason it's for sale instead of sitting on my laptop.
